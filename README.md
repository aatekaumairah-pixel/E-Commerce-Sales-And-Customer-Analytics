# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

Excel-based analytics project on a 6,000-order e-commerce dataset — data cleaning, formula-based analysis, PivotTables, and an interactive dashboard answering 29 business questions.

## Project Overview

**Objective:** Analyze e-commerce transactional data and build a decision-ready dashboard answering key business questions on sales performance, profitability, and customer insights.

**Dataset:** 6,000 order-level records including customer demographics, product details, sales, discounts, payment methods, and delivery information.

**Process:**
1. **Data Cleaning** — Standardized city names, payment methods, and customer names; handled missing/inconsistent values
2. **Formula-Based Analysis** — Calculated Net Sales, Total Cost, Profit, and Profit Margin using Excel formulas
3. **Pivot Analysis** — Built PivotTables to break down sales and profit by category, product, customer segment, and time period
4. **Dashboard Design** — Created an interactive dashboard with KPI cards, trend charts, bar/pie charts, and slicers for dynamic filtering

## Business Questions Answered

**Formula-based (F01–F10):** Total net sales, total profit, profit margin, order count, unique customers, AOV, category-wise sales (SUMIFS), segment-wise average spend (AVERAGEIFS), product price lookup (XLOOKUP), profit-margin classification (IF).

**Pivot-based (P01–P12):** Highest sales/profit month, top category by revenue & profit, top 10 products by sales, high-sales/low-margin products, top revenue-generating customer segment, top 10 customers by spending, top state/city by sales, most-used payment method, average delivery time by shipping mode.

**Dashboard (D01–D06):** KPI cards, monthly sales trend, category-wise sales & profit, top-performing products, customer segment contribution, discount vs. profitability relationship.
